# 🎣 BaitLogic

**Beyond the Bite. Protect What Matters.**

BaitLogic is a **woman-owned local outdoor intelligence platform** built around four connected pillars:

1. **Fishing Intelligence** — weather, barometric pressure, water conditions, patterns, catches, and practical local fishing decisions.
2. **Community Knowledge** — firsthand Field Checks, reports, observations, and useful experience shared by people who are actually outside.
3. **Water & Environment** — water health, habitat, wildlife, access, and verified public environmental data that help explain the bigger local picture.
4. **Conservation Action** — clear guidance for noticing, documenting, and reporting concerns to the correct public authority so people can help protect the resources they use.

Fishing is the foundation, but BaitLogic goes **beyond the bite** by connecting the fish, the water, the conditions, the community, and the responsibility to protect the places that make outdoor life possible.

## What BaitLogic stands for

**Understand the water. Learn from the community. Fish smarter. Protect what matters.**

BaitLogic exists to make useful outdoor knowledge easier to access, easier to understand, and easier to act on. Core fishing, community, environmental, and conservation information remains free and is not placed behind a paywall.

BaitLogic does **not** fabricate live data, invent community activity, or present community observations as official scientific or agency records. When verified information is unavailable, the product should say so clearly.

## Product hierarchy

Everything in BaitLogic should support one or more of the four core pillars above. Supporting outdoor categories such as hiking, paddling, camping, wildlife viewing, habitat, and access are welcome where they strengthen the local intelligence system, but they are **supporting categories—not separate competing identities**.

The product should feel like one connected system:

**Conditions → Community Knowledge → Better Outdoor/Fishing Decisions → Conservation Action**
