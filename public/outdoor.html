No single activity defines BaitLogic. The useful signals change depending on what you are actually trying to do.

LOCAL SIGNALS

The things that can change your plan

Weather

Temperature, wind, rain, storms

Current outside conditions and short-term changes.

Air

Air quality & smoke

Useful for long days outside, kids, older adults and respiratory concerns.

Daylight

Sunrise, sunset & usable light

Helps plan timing, safety and how long you can realistically stay out.

Community

What people are actually seeing

Access issues, wildlife, habitat changes, trail conditions, water changes and local observations.

ACTIVITY-SPECIFIC

Only show the extra signals when they matter

Fishing can use pressure and water data. Paddling can use flow and wind. Hiking can prioritize heat, storms, daylight and access. Camping can emphasize overnight conditions. Wildlife watching can prioritize dawn/dusk and community sightings.

Access

Trail, shoreline & public-land notes

Community-reported closures, blocked access, muddy conditions and practical local context.

Wildlife

Recent sightings & seasonal behavior

Community observations that help people notice patterns without pretending every sighting is verified science.

Conservation

Problems worth acting on

Trash, habitat damage, invasive species, pollution and other issues that need attention or official reporting.

Water

Optional when relevant

Flow, gage height, temperature and other water signals for fishing, paddling and waterside activities—not the center of the whole platform.

PEOPLE COMPLETE THE PICTURE

Public data cannot tell you everything happening outside.

BaitLogic becomes more useful when people report what they actually see: wildlife movement, trail problems, blocked access, trash, habitat changes, unusual conditions, fishing patterns and conservation concerns.
