Beyond the Bite. Protect What Matters.

Notice it. Document it. Report it.

Anglers are often the first people to notice changing water, dead fish, pollution, illegal dumping, invasive species, damaged habitat, or unsafe access.

BaitLogic Nature-Check

Leave the water better than you found it.

Pick up fishing line, plastic, cans, bait containers, or other litter when it is safe to do so. Take a photo for your own record and share the action with other anglers to normalize stewardship.

When Something Is Wrong
1

Observe

Note the waterbody, approximate location, time, smell, color, dead wildlife, discharge, algae, or other unusual conditions.

2

Document

Take photos or video from a safe location. Do not touch unknown chemicals, dead wildlife, or hazardous material.

3

Report

Use the responsible state agency so the report reaches people who can investigate it.

Official Reporting

Illinois

Illinois EPA accepts pollution complaints including illegal discharges into streams and rivers and other threats to water and public health.

Illinois Pollution Complaint

Missouri

Missouri Department of Conservation provides reporting guidance for fish kills and water-quality problems.
