LESS THAN 1% REMAINS.

WE DON'T GET ANOTHER ILLINOIS.

Prairies once covered roughly two-thirds of Illinois. HeartLands Conservancy reports that less than 1% of original Illinois prairie remains. The response cannot stop at awareness: protect remnants, restore habitat, support the people doing the work, and make it easier for ordinary residents to act.

Why this belongs here

Healthy land and healthy water are the same conservation story. Prairie roots hold soil, absorb rainfall and reduce erosion. Habitat protection supports wildlife, watersheds and the outdoor places our communities depend on.

Illinois DNR notes that dense prairie sod helps conserve both soil and water.
