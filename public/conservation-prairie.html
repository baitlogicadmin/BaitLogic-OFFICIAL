BaitLogic Community Action #001

LESS THAN 1% REMAINS.

WE DON'T GET ANOTHER ILLINOIS.

Prairies once covered roughly two-thirds of Illinois. HeartLands Conservancy reports that less than 1% of original Illinois prairie remains. This first BaitLogic Community Action makes the next step immediate: learn what is being lost, support people already restoring it, volunteer if you can, and put the facts in front of someone else.

First local focus

Knoll Wildlife Sanctuary

Madison County, Illinois · 40 acres · rare sand prairie and sand savanna. HeartLands Conservancy owns and manages the property and says active work includes invasive-species removal, native prairie establishment, pond-edge restoration, wildlife habitat protection, and future public access planning.

GO DIRECTLY TO KNOLL PROJECT

BaitLogic is highlighting and supporting this existing conservation effort. This page does not claim a formal partnership with HeartLands Conservancy.

Why this belongs here

Healthy land and healthy water are the same conservation story. Prairie roots protect soil and water, while prairie habitat supports pollinators, birds, amphibians, reptiles and the outdoor places our communities depend on.
